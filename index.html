    PolyTrack 

    PolyTrack is a time-trial racing game built around improving your performance with every attempt. Each run highlights mistakes from the previous one, pushing you to adjust your timing, control, and racing line.

    At first, the goal is simply to reach the finish. After a few attempts, your focus shifts to cutting down small time losses. You start recognizing how minor errors like entering a corner too quickly can affect your overall run.Taking the first turn too fast might force a restart. On the next attempt, braking earlier can save valuable time and improve your flow.

    Instead of competing against other drivers, you are constantly trying to beat your own best record. Every run helps you gain better control and consistency across the track.You can start instantly in your browser and work on mastering each track.

    How PolyTrack Works

    Each track is designed around repetition and improvement. You replay the same sections, identify where you lost speed, and immediately try again with small adjustments.

    Even slight changes like braking earlier, turning tighter, or landing straighter can reduce your time by fractions of a second. Since runs are short, most of your progress comes from retrying and refining mistakes rather than long races.

    Leaderboards and ghost replays show how faster runs are achieved, helping you understand where time can be saved. Over time, repeated practice turns inconsistent runs into smooth and reliable performances.

    Controls

    Desktop:

    W / A / S / D or Arrow Keys – Drive
    T – Restart run
    R – Restart from checkpoint
    C – Switch to first-person view
    Spacebar – Pause

    Editor Controls

    Left Click – Place or build items
    Right Click + Move Mouse – Move camera view
    Middle Mouse Button + Move – Rotate camera view
    Mouse Scroll – Zoom in / out
    Shift + Mouse Scroll / Z / C – Adjust height up / down
    Q / E – Rotate camera left / right
    R / Space – Rotate selected item
    X – Delete item
    T – Test drive track

    Mobile:

    On-screen controls – Steering and actions

    Competing with Other Players

    Competition is based on time comparison rather than direct racing. You improve by analyzing leaderboard times and ghost replays from faster runs.

    Watching how others handle corners and sections helps you identify where you are losing speed and what adjustments can improve your performance.Small improvements in control and positioning can make a noticeable difference in your final time.

    PolyTrack Maps

    The game includes 13 official tracks along with 30+ community-created maps across different environments like Summer, Winter, and Desert.

    Each track requires a different driving approach:

    •  Smooth steering for long corners
    •  Precise braking on tight sections
    •  Accurate landings on jumps

    As you replay tracks, your driving becomes more controlled and predictable, allowing you to consistently reduce your time.

    Build Your Own Tracks

    The track editor allows you to create custom maps using different building pieces.

    •  Select and place track elements
    •  Rotate parts to shape the route
    •  Add checkpoints and a finish line

    You can test your track at any time and adjust difficulty based on your design. Once complete, tracks can be exported and shared for others to try and beat your time.

    Playing Custom Tracks

    Community-made tracks offer additional challenges beyond the official maps.Import a track code to access it in the Custom Tracks section. These maps range from simple high-speed layouts to more complex designs that require precision driving.

    Custom tracks provide endless variations and new ways to improve your skills.

    Tips to Improve Your Time

    •  Brake before corners – Late braking reduces control and slows you down
    •  Land straight after jumps – Angled landings waste momentum
    •  Keep tight racing lines – Shorter paths improve speed
    •  Approach platforms directly – Side angles can break momentum
    •  Stay low on wall rides – Better exit speed and control
    •  Restart quickly after mistakes – Repetition builds consistency

    About PolyTrack

    PolyTrack was developed by Kodub as a time-trial racing experience focused on skill improvement. It draws inspiration from games like Trackmania, emphasizing precision over visuals.The simple 3D block-style graphics keep attention on driving performance rather than visual detail.

    FAQ

    How do you get faster times in PolyTrack?


    Drive smoothly, brake early, land straight, and repeat sections to reduce mistakes.

    Why do I lose speed after jumps?


    Landing at an angle forces correction, which reduces momentum.

    Should I restart after making a mistake?


    Yes. Restarting quickly helps you improve specific sections faster.

    How do you find the fastest route?


    Watch ghost runs, test different lines, and compare results to identify time-saving paths.
